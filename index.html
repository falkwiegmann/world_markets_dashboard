Markets dashboard

About. This page began as a personal educational project and remains one: a non-commercial hobby dashboard for learning about markets and data visualisation, shared in case it is useful to others. It carries no advertising, charges nothing, and sells nothing.

Data sources. Index, FX, commodity and crypto quotes via Yahoo Finance (unofficial API; end-of-day and delayed values). US Treasury yields: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (H.15), retrieved via FRED®, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Euro-area 10Y yield: Source: European Central Bank (AAA-rated euro area government bond yield curve). US home prices: FHFA House Price Index (purchase-only), via FRED. International home prices: Source: Bank for International Settlements, residential property price statistics, via FRED. S&P 500 valuation series (EPS, P/E, CAPE, dividend yield): Robert Shiller's published dataset and multpl.com.

Derived figures. All ratios, spreads, indexed-to-100 comparisons, and the daily interpolation of monthly valuation series are computed by this page from the sources above. They are this site's own calculations, not official published values, and may differ from the providers' figures.

All data is provided “as is”, for personal, informational purposes only. It may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate, and must not be used for trading or investment decisions.
